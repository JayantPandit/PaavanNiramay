Featured Technology

Computerized Spinal Decompression Machine

Our clinic is equipped with advanced motorized spinal decompression therapy machines. Unlike ordinary traction, this technology delivers precise, logarithmic distraction force to target specific collapsed disc segments (L4-L5, L5-S1, or Cervical discs).

1

Negative Intra-Discal Pressure (Vacuum Effect)

Pulls protruding, bulged disc material back into place away from the spinal nerve root.

2

Nutrient & Fluid Imbibition

Increases the flow of essential water, oxygen, and nutrients into the disc to accelerate natural healing.

3

Safe, Relaxing & Pain-Free

Comfortable treatment sessions without any surgical incisions, hospital stays, or downtime.
